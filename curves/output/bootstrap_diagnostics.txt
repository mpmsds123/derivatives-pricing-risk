  step at the handoff  : +73.8 bp

  The step is the futures/swap basis landing on a short stub. Part of it
  is genuine convexity (futures are margined daily, swaps are not, so
  futures rates sit above forwards), part is that the two quote sets are
  separate market pulls. It is the price of a piecewise-constant forward:
  a smooth interpolator would spread it, at the cost of a global solve.
